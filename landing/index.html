How it works

Four steps. A minute to set up.

1

Set your goal + sub-goal

Daily goal 313 by default. Sub-goal defaults to 30 — change it to whatever feels right today.

2

Start a session

Hit start and the timer runs. Your pace updates live as you work.

3

Add a comment per post

Pick the platform, note what you posted, save. Keep the window open to chain comments.

4

Watch milestones

1% is a win. 25% is a wave. 100% of your sub-goal is the day done right.
